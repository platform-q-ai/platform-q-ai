Welcome to your new static site built with Alkali! This is your first post, and it's a great place to start sharing your thoughts, ideas, and stories with the world.

## Getting Started

Here are a few things you can do to get started:

- **Edit this post** - Open `content/posts/YYYY-MM-DD-welcome.md` and make it your own
- **Create new posts** - Run `mix alkali.post "Your Post Title"` to create new content
- **Customize your site** - Edit `config/alkali.exs` to change your site title, author, and more
- **Modify layouts** - Check out the files in `layouts/` to change how your site looks
