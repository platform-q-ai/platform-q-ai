Every experiment we run keeps a living notebook. Hypotheses are marked supported, refuted or withdrawn as results come in, and nothing is deleted, so you can see how our view changed and why.

Each claim in a notebook has an ID, a status and links to the evidence behind it and to the claims it supports or refutes. We are preparing an open API so that other labs, and their agents, can query the notebooks and cite the exact claim an answer comes from.
